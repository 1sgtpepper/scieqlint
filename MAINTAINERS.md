## Maintainer duties

Maintainers are responsible for:

- preserving the product contract,
- keeping release scope narrow,
- labeling issues consistently,
- reviewing contributor PRs respectfully,
- updating docs and fixtures with behavior changes,
- publishing release notes with deferred scope listed clearly.

## Review rule

Do not widen a contributor's PR. If a related idea is useful, open a follow-up issue.
