# Code of Conduct

SciEqLint aims to be a calm, respectful, technically rigorous open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and specific.
- Assume good intent, but correct harmful behavior promptly.
- Keep feedback actionable.
- Respect contributor time and scope.
- Make room for scientific writers, students, maintainers, and first-time OSS contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Dismissive comments about a contributor's background or skill level.
- Repeatedly widening a contributor's scoped work after maintainers agreed on scope.
- Publishing private contact or security details without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments; close issues; block users; or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`.
